ProFix AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Welcome to ProFix AI, the smart labour booking application connecting customers with skilled professionals. Your privacy is critical to maintaining the trust of our dual-user ecosystem (Customers and Labourers).

This Privacy Policy explains:

  • What data we collect from Customers and Labourers.
  • How we use location tracking and AI data.
  • How we share information to facilitate bookings.
  • Your rights and security measures.

1. Data We Collect

1.1 User & Labourer Identity

To create a trusted environment, we collect:

  • Identity Information: Name, phone number, and email address for account creation.
  • Verification Documents (Labourers Only): Government ID or skill certification for background verification purposes.
  • Profile Photos: Used to help customers and labourers identify each other.

1.2 Service & Booking Data

When you book or accept a service, we process:

  • Service Details: Type of work (e.g., Plumbing, Electrical), task description, and photos of the work area.
  • Location Data: precise GPS location to match nearby labourers and track arrival in real-time.
  • Transaction Records: Booking history, pricing, and payment status (financial details are processed by secure payment gateways).

1.3 AI Chatbot Interactions

  • Chat Logs: Text inputs sent to our AI smart chatbot are processed to provide instant support and booking assistance.
  • Usage Patterns: Common queries are analyzed to improve the AI's accuracy.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is essential for the ProFix AI platform to function:

  • Connecting Users: Matching customers with the nearest available skilled labourer based on geolocation.
  • Real-Time Tracking: enabling customers to track the live location of the labourer en route to the job site.
  • Safety & Verification: Validating labourer credentials via the Admin Dashboard to ensure platform safety.
  • AI Assistance: Utilizing query data to help the chatbot answer service-related questions instantly.

3. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We share minimal necessary data to facilitate services:

  • Between Users: Once a booking is confirmed, the Customer and Labourer can see each other's name, photo, and location.
  • Third-Party Services: Location data is shared with map providers (e.g., Google Maps) for navigation.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose data if required by law or to protect user safety.

4. Data Security

We employ industry-standard security measures:

  • Encryption: All sensitive data, including chat logs and location streams, is encrypted in transit and at rest.
  • Access Control: Only verified Admin Dashboard personnel can access sensitive verification documents.
  • Secure Infrastructure: Hosted on secure cloud servers with regular security audits.

5. Your Rights

  • Profile Management: Labourers and Customers can update their profiles via the app interface.
  • Location Permissions: You can toggle location services on/off in your device settings (though this may affect booking functionality).
  • Account Deletion: You may request account deletion via the app settings; legal transaction records may be retained.

6. Third-Party Integrations

  • Google Maps API: For location tracking and address selection.
  • AI Service Providers: For processing chatbot queries.
  • Payment Gateways: For secure processing of service payments.

7. Children's Privacy

ProFix AI is strictly for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.
